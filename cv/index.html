Professional Summary

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Robotics at DECAR research group, McGill University, Canada. My research interests include robot autonomy, specifically in the areas of robot perception, navigation, and mapping. I am passionate about developing algorithms and systems that enable robots to operate effectively in complex and dynamic environments.
