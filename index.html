John Kyungmin Jung

DECAR Group, McGill University. Montreal, Canada.

I am a Ph.D. candidate at DECAR research group at McGill University, specializing in robot perception and navigation under the supervision of Professor James Richard Forbes. Specifically, my research focuses on developing robust simultaneous localization and mapping (SLAM) algorithms for autonomous underwater vehicles operating in challenging environments. My work involves designing sensor fusion techniques, probabilistic mapping methods, and real-time navigation strategies to enhance the capabilities of underwater robots in complex and dynamic scenarios.
